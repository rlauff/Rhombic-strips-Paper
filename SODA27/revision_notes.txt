
--Results section moved up--:   Paper Structure and Merit (All Reviewers) All reviewers rightly pointed out that the formal statements of our results appear too late in the manuscript. We completely agree that this must be changed. We recognize that our current layout obscured the broader impact of the paper, causing its merit to be understated. While rhombic strips are a fairly new concept, in this work they are used to answer almost all cases of a 30-year-old open question by Ruskey and Savage concerning fundamental objects in combinatorics. Furthermore, they are essential to our result on tree-associahedra, which extends the findings of a previous SODA publication and represents the most general result regarding facet-Hamiltonian cycles in graph associahedra to date. We believe this is a substantial contribution to the field.

--Nothing changed--:    Novelty of the Constructions (Reviewer B) Reviewer B correctly notes that our constructions abstract the techniques used by Behrooznia et al. However, extending these techniques from the hypercube to products of general graded posets—and combining them in a novel way—is exactly what enables our new applications.

Lattice Condition and SJT Citation (Reviewer C)

--Added two sentences explaining this--:    Lattice condition: Reviewer C asked why (R) must be a lattice in the definition of rhombic strips. Without this condition, the outer face may touch too many elements of (R) (for example, a strip without any edges would then incorrectly be considered a valid rhombic strip). We found this to be the simplest condition to restrict this, and we will explicitly clarify this reasoning in the text.
